A's Child.

In one of My Dreams, there's one Dream that I am really thankful to God for.

I guess I forgot how it started, but I still want to make this exist on the page.

A and the 1-year-old baby, and with someone who was 19 years old at the beginning.

The sun rising in the morning, there's someone who suddenly became a good person (someone who suddenly became motivated and determined toward achieving something). That person has a baby, let's call that person is A. A really wanted the baby to not become like the parent, like A. So, A started doing what A wanted. A raised the baby with full of love, care, and determination. Not even a single "give up" ever came to A's mind. What a serious person.

A wasn't like this before, from being lazy to becoming someone who was passionate about achieving something within themselves, because of the one thing A had: the baby A had in A's hands.

Everything A did, A could imagine what the baby would look like in the near future, it's like A could see the future of the baby would like. So A was always changing and upgrading A's plans to make things better for the baby.

While A carried this strong determination to achieve something, A took the baby outside home with confidence, bringing that positive mindset outside and socializing with people A knew, the people who used to accompany A when A was a child.

And they finally arrived at a hometown where A had once lived as a child back then. Now, A and baby, was warmly and joyfully welcomed by A's friends and the people A had met during childhood.

A realized how A had treated them so bad back then. A apologized for what A did and never visiting them, when A was invited to come. A said that A would make it up for what A had done to them, even if it couldn't be 100%. A dared to make that decision because of the second total change in A's life, caused by the baby A had.

If that baby didn't exist, A would probably have continued drowning deeper into themselves.

Then they're all went to a small mosque. The welcome from everyone there became even warmer and more full of love. They entered the mosque and sat there happily, with a small welcoming speech. Before the prayer, they prayed together, led by the mosque's caretaker, for A and A's baby.

At that moment, A was extremely thankful to God, to the baby, to A's life partner, to A's parents, to A's siblings, and to everyone who had been involved in A's life.

Once again, A was truly, deeply thankful from the bottom of A's heart.

Without the baby and A's life partner, A would have been nothing more than a miserable man living without a purpose.
